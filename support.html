How can we help?

Alarms, reminders, Quiet Apps, Apple Health or Snoozly Pro: here are the fixes for the most common questions.

  • My alarm didn’t ring
  • Reminders aren’t arriving
  • Quiet Apps isn’t pausing my apps
  • Snoozly can’t see my sleep in Apple Health
  • My widget isn’t updating
  • I bought Snoozly Pro, but it’s locked
  • How do I cancel Snoozly Pro?
  • I only want to change tonight

My alarm didn’t ring

  1. In the Settings app, open Snoozly and make sure Alarms are allowed. If they’re off, Snoozly shows a warning on Tonight.
  2. In Snoozly, open Schedule and check that the morning has a wake-up time and its alarm is on.
  3. Check that tonight wasn’t skipped. Skipping a night turns off its alarm until you wake.
  4. The alarm rings even when your iPhone is on silent, so a missed alarm is almost always a permission or a schedule setting.

Reminders aren’t arriving

  1. In the Settings app, open Notifications → Snoozly and allow notifications.
  2. In Snoozly, check that reminders are on and not paused.
  3. A Focus such as Sleep can hold notifications back. Allow Snoozly in that Focus if you want wind-down nudges to come through.

Quiet Apps isn’t pausing my apps

  1. Quiet Apps needs Screen Time access. If you declined it, turn Quiet Apps off and on again in Snoozly to be asked again.
  2. Choose at least one app or category. With nothing chosen, nothing is paused.
  3. Apps are paused from wind-down until you wake, only on nights that are on in your schedule and not skipped.

Snoozly can’t see my sleep in Apple Health

  1. Open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps → Snoozly, and turn on Sleep.
  2. Apple Health needs sleep data to share. Wear Apple Watch to bed, or turn on a Sleep Schedule in the Health app.
  3. Last night can take a little while to appear after you wake up.

My widget isn’t updating

  1. Open Snoozly once so the widget has your latest schedule.
  2. iOS decides how often widgets refresh, so changes can take a few minutes to show.
  3. To add a widget, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Snoozly.
  4. The wind-down countdown, weekly schedule and weekly summary widgets are part of Snoozly Pro.

I bought Snoozly Pro, but it’s locked

  1. In Snoozly, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
  2. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  3. Subscribed on another iPhone? Restore Purchases unlocks Pro on this one too, with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel Snoozly Pro?

  1. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Snoozly.
  2. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.
  3. Refunds are handled by Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I only want to change tonight

  1. On Tonight, touch and hold the tonight card and choose Tonight Settings to change tonight’s bedtime or wake-up. Your weekly schedule stays the same, and everything goes back after you wake up.
  2. To take the night off, tap Skip Tonight. Reminders, quiet apps and the alarm are off for tonight only.
