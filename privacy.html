Our approach

Snoozly is built to work without an account, and to keep what you tell it on your iPhone. The developer of Snoozly does not collect, receive or store your personal information. This policy explains what the app uses, why, and how you stay in control.

What stays on your iPhone

To plan your nights, Snoozly saves the following on your device:

  • Your sleep schedule: bedtimes, wind-down times, wake-up times, alarms and snooze settings, and any gradual bedtime shift.
  • Your bedtime and morning routines, and which steps you check off.
  • Your morning check-ins: how you felt, how you slept, the hours you slept, the habits you tagged, and the score worked out from them.
  • Your profile name, icon or photo, and your app preferences.

This information is shared only with Snoozly’s own widgets, Live Activities and extensions on the same device, through an app group. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Snoozly asks to read your sleep data, and nothing else. It uses that data on your device to show how long and how well you slept, your deep and REM sleep, and when you fell asleep and woke up. Snoozly never writes to Apple Health.

Health data is not used for advertising or marketing, is not shared with third parties, and never leaves your device through Snoozly. You can stop sharing at any time in the Health app: tap your profile, then Apps, then Snoozly.

Screen Time and Quiet Apps

Quiet Apps uses Apple’s Screen Time to pause the apps and categories you choose, from wind-down until you wake. Apple keeps your activity private: Snoozly receives only an anonymous token for each app you pick, so it can’t see which apps they are or what you do in them. Snoozly does not read your screen time or usage history. You can remove Snoozly’s Screen Time access at any time in the Settings app.

Notifications, alarms and sleep sounds

Wind-down and bedtime reminders, the check-in reminder, and your wake-up alarm are scheduled on your device using Apple’s notification and alarm services. Sleep sounds play on your device. None of this needs an Internet connection or sends information to us.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a profile photo, Snoozly uses the camera or the photo you pick only to create that picture. The photo is saved on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

Snoozly Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes your payment and manages your subscription; we never receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device to unlock Pro features.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

When you use Snoozly with Siri, Shortcuts, Control Center, widgets or Live Activities, those features are provided by Apple and run on your device. Apple handles any data involved under its own privacy policy.

What we don’t do

Snoozly has no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We don’t sell, rent or share your personal information, and we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Your control

You can edit or delete check-ins and routines in the app, change your profile at any time, and turn off Apple Health, Screen Time, notifications, alarms and camera access in the Settings app. Deleting Snoozly removes its data from your device. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple may include Snoozly’s data in that backup, under your control.

Children

Snoozly is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If Snoozly changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated and the date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
